## Research

My research focuses on **modeling and understanding robot dynamics and developing control algorithms** for agile and unconventional robotic systems, including jumping robots, swimming robots, and tethered aerial robots. I combine physical modeling, classical control, and reinforcement learning to develop motions that are agile, efficient, and adaptive.

I'm particularly interested in how a robot's mechanics can become part of the solution to a control problem. My work spans momentum-driven locomotion and learning-based control, connecting models and simulation with experiments on physical robots. More recently, I've begun exploring **whole-body control of humanoid robots**, with an emphasis on dynamics-aware control using reinforcement learning.

## Building robots

I enjoy building complete robotic systems from the ground up, integrating mechanical design, electronics, sensing, and control. Working across the robotics stack lets me connect ideas about dynamics and learning with the practical details of making a robot work.

## Selected work

- [SpinJORR]({{ '/research/spinjorr/' | relative_url }}): A compact robot that rolls, steers, jumps, and self-rights using internal rotors and momentum-based actuation.
- [Learning to control an agile robotic fish]({{ '/research/agile-fish/' | relative_url }}): Adaptive PID control learned in a differentiable simulator and transferred to a swimming robot for simultaneous speed and path tracking.
